# Production Overlay

Compact on-screen windows that show production, consumption and stock levels of the items and fluids you care about, so you can spot what's falling behind at a glance.

A Factorio 2.0 mod. Works with and without Space Age.

## Features

- Per-minute production, consumption and balance for any item or fluid. The balance turns red when you consume more than you make.
- Stock bars: set a target amount for an item and a small bar shows how full your storage is (red, then yellow, then green on target, purple when over).
- Counts logistic network storage and Space Age platform hubs. Use all networks or only the one you're standing in (or looking at).
- Multiple independent windows, each with its own item list, surface (current, everywhere, or a specific planet or platform) and network mode.
- Pinned mode: a compact, semi-transparent, click-through overlay.
- Quality support: tracks the exact quality you pick. Works with third-party quality mods.
- Read-only and multiplayer-safe: never changes your game, only its own UI.

## Usage

1. Click the bar-chart button on the shortcut bar, or press `Ctrl+Alt+P`, to show or hide the overlay.
2. Click the empty slot at the bottom of a window and pick an item or fluid. For items, enter a target stock (or 0 for no bar) and press Enter.
3. Left-click an icon to replace it, right-click to remove it, use the arrows to reorder rows.
4. Click a stock bar to change its target.
5. Use "+" for another window and the dropdown to choose the surface.
6. Click the pushpin, or press `Ctrl+Alt+O` to pin all windows, for the compact click-through view. Click the pin again to unpin.

Hotkeys can be rebound in the controls settings. The update interval is a map setting.

## Installation

Install from the in-game mod browser, or download a release zip and place it in your Factorio `mods` folder.
